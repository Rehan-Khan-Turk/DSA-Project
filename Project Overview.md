Introduction:

The “Car Sell & Purchase Management System” is a software project designed to manage the buying and selling of cars in an organized way. The system allows users to add car records, view available cars, search for specific cars, purchase cars, and manage car information.The project applies Data Structures and Algorithms (DSA) concepts to store, search, sort, and manage car records efficiently. It provides a simple and organized solution for managing car sales and purchases.
Project Objectives:
The main objectives of the project are:
1.To develop a system for managing car selling and purchasing.
2.To store car information in an organized data structure.
3.To allow users to add and display car records.
4.To provide a searching facility for finding cars.
5.To implement sorting for organizing car records.
6.To manage car purchase and selling operations.
7.To apply Data Structures and Algorithms concepts in a practical project.
8.To analyse the efficiency of the algorithms used.
Scope of the project:
The scope of the “Car Sell & Purchase Management System” includes managing basic car buying and selling activities.
The system will allow users to:
1.Add new cars to the system.
2.Display available cars.
3.Search for a car using relevant information.
4.Sort cars according to selected information such as price or model.
5.Purchase an available car.
6.Sell or remove a car from the available list.
7.Maintain basic transaction records.
Conclusion:
The “Car Sell and Purchase Management System” will provide a centralized solution for managing vehicles, customers, purchases, and payments in a car dealership. The project addresses practical problems associated with manual or disconnected record management. Through the collection of original evidence such as questionnaires or interviews, our group will validate the identified problems and refine the system requirements. The proposed system will provide a foundation for a more organized, efficient, and reliable car purchasing process.
